Apply David Allen's natural planning model to the project. Write a purpose, up to five principles, an outcome vision one paragraph long, a brainstorm of up to twelve concrete pieces, and exactly three next physical actions each starting with a verb, with a context and minutes for each. Contexts come from the vocabulary given. Be concrete and short. Each nextActions entry is the action text only; put the context in contexts and the minutes in minutes, never inside the action text.
